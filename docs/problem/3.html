There is a uniform magnetic field directed perpendicular to the plane of Fig. 1. Its magnitude is $B=kv$ where $k$ is a positive constant. Particle A has charge $+q$, particle $B$ has charge $-q$, and both particles have mass $m$. The charges are sufficiently small that the electrostatic force between the particles is negligible compared to the magnetic force exerted by the external field. The particles are launched simultaneously from the positions shown in Figs. 1 and 2, with the indicated initial velocities. All collisions between the particles are perfectly elastic. It is known that when $v=1$, particle $B$ passes through the point $(-\ell,0)$ in Fig. 1 while moving in the negative $y$-direction. Find all values of $v>0$ for which particle $B$ passes through $(-\ell,0)$ while moving in the negative $y$-direction.
